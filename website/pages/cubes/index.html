CSS-3D scene

Look: hold the right mouse button and drag. Move: WASD, Space up / Shift down. Edit: left-click a block to select (Shift-click for multi-select), drag a gizmo handle to move it (yellow handle rotates). Place: click + Cube/Wall/Ramp, move over the grid floor, click to drop. Group: G groups the selection, U ungroups. Delete removes, Esc cancels. Camera toggles free-fly / orbit (orbit: right-drag to swing, wheel to zoom). Export copies portable markup.
