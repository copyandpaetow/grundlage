CSS-3D scene

Look: hold Alt and drag. Move: WASD, Space up / Shift down. Edit: left-click a block to select — a gizmo wraps it; drag a handle to move (yellow handle rotates). ⌘/Ctrl-click adds more blocks for grouping. Set size with the palette inputs. Place: click + Cube/Wall/Ramp, move over the grid floor, click to drop. Group: G groups, U ungroups. Delete removes, Esc cancels / deselects. Camera toggles free-fly / orbit (orbit: Alt-drag to swing, wheel to zoom). Export copies portable markup.

Compare: the bench grid at 100 · 500 · 1000 · 2000 cubes, as a lattice, a cube of cubes, at 125 · 1000 · 2197 cubes, as a solid lattice at 1000 cubes, or the same scene without grundlage on the vanilla page.
